Keep your lights on.

Booklyn turns your semester into a city at night. Study a unit, pass a short check on your own material, and a window lights up. Let a week slide and it slowly fades.

Open BooklynSee how it works

Free. No account needed. Your files never leave your device.

One city. One window per week.

Your degree is the city, a semester is a district, a module is a building and each teaching unit is a floor.

1

Add your material

Drop PDFs, Word and PowerPoint files or whole folders. Booklyn sorts them into the right module and week. Files stay in your browser.

2

Study a floor

Open the week, read, keep notes and use the focus timer. No pressure, no streak to lose in one bad day.

3

Pass the check

Short questions are made from your own files, on your device. Pass and the light goes on, and a guest moves in who pays rent in coins.

Made for a real semester

Everything a student actually needs, nothing you have to configure for an hour.

Questions from your files

Fill-in and multiple-choice questions are generated locally from your slides and scripts. Optional AI questions are off until you turn them on.

Deadlines in one place

Exams, tests and hand-ins sit next to your city, and the next one shows up first.

Works offline

Install it on your phone or laptop and study on the train. Progress syncs only if you choose to.

Sync without email

Optional sync uses a secret code, not an email or a password. Lose the code and nobody, including us, can get your progress back, so keep it safe.

ILIAS connector

An optional browser extension finds new files on your HSLU ILIAS and lets you add them with one click. It uses your own sign-in, nothing is uploaded.

Make it yours

Coins from your lit floors buy trees, pools, helipads and seasons. Only cosmetics, never real money.

Private by design

  • Your files, notes and progress are stored in your own browser.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, no third-party fonts or scripts.
  • Sync is optional and stores only your progress, never your files.
  • You can export a backup or delete everything with one button.
