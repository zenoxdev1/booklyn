How it works

Your degree is the city, a semester is a district, a module is a building and each teaching unit is a floor.

1

Add your material

Drop PDFs, Word and PowerPoint files or whole folders. Booklyn suggests the right module and week from the file name, and you confirm. Files stay in your browser.

2

Study a floor

Open the week, read, keep notes and use the focus timer. No streak to lose in one bad day.

3

Pass the check

Short questions are made from your own files, on your device. Pass and the light goes on, and a guest moves in who pays you rent in coins.

The rules in 30 seconds

💡A light lasts 7 daysThen it fades for 7 more days. Pass the check again to bring it back. Lights you save in time last longer (14, then 30 days).
🏠Guests pay rentEvery lit floor pays 1 coin a day. Rent is collected when you open the campus, and it stores up to 3 days.
📌Pin easy floorsA pinned light never fades, but it pays no rent. Good for units that will not be in the exam.
🛡️ShieldsA shield saves a fading light once. You can hold two.
🎁Bonuses+10 for the first light, +15 when a week is lit in every module, +50 for a finished building.
🎨Coins are cosmeticSpend them on trees, pools, helipads and seasons. They never cost real money.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no real-money purchases.

Who can see my files?

Only you. Files are stored in your browser on your own device and are never uploaded. If you turn on sync, only your progress is stored, never your files.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any modern phone browser, and you can install it to your home screen. The ILIAS connector needs a computer with Edge or Chrome.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit it opens without a connection. Sync catches up when you are back online.

What if I lose my sync code?

It cannot be recovered, because we store only a one-way hash of it. Your data on your device is untouched. Write the code down somewhere safe.

Is the AI reading my files?

Not by default. Questions are generated on your device. If you switch on the optional AI setting, short excerpts of the unit you are checking are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to write questions, and are not stored.

Is this an official HSLU app?

No. Booklyn is an unofficial student project and is not affiliated with HSLU.

Private by design

  • Your files, notes and progress are stored in your own browser.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, no third-party fonts or scripts.
  • Sync is optional and stores only your progress, never your files.
  • You can export a backup or delete everything with one button.
