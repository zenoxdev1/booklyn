3. Your material

Only add material you are allowed to use. Course documents are usually protected by copyright. Booklyn keeps them on your device and does not share them. Do not use Booklyn to copy or pass on material to others.

4. ILIAS connector

The optional extension works with your own HSLU account. Use it only as your school allows, and stop using it if HSLU asks you to. We are not responsible for changes on ILIAS that stop it from working.

5. Sync codes and backups

A sync code is the only key to your synced progress. Keep it secret. If you lose it, it cannot be recovered. Booklyn stores your data in your browser, which can be cleared by you or your device, so please export a backup regularly.
